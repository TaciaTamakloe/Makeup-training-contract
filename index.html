Makeup Artistry Training Agreement

Professional Certification Course

1. Training Scope & Expectations

The training session is an intensive 7-working-day program. The Trainee will receive hands-on instruction in skin preparation, color theory, foundation blending, highlighting/contouring, and bridal glam techniques.

2. Logistics: Models & Products

  • Products: High-end makeup products and tools will be provided for use on-site only during training hours.
  • Models: Live models will be provided for demonstration. Trainees may bring a personal model for practice sessions with 24-hour prior notice to the Trainer.

3. Attendance & Punctuality

Trainees must be available and on time for all sessions. Training starts promptly at the scheduled time. Missed hours will not be rescheduled, and consistent tardiness may result in the withholding of the training certificate.

4. Payment Terms

The total cost of the training is Five Hundred Ghana Cedis (500 GHS). Full payment must be completed before the commencement of the first session. All payments are non-refundable.
